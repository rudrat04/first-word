My story

I wanted to be a geologist.
Blame the movie 2012.

I grew up in Mumbai, in a middle class family. As a kid I wanted to be a geologist, thanks to the movie 2012. I ended up studying AI and Machine Learning instead.

After college I joined mple.ai as a Product Manager and Founder's Associate. I got curious about outbound and how it really works, so I fixed the outbound engine and built an AI brief around it. I didn't study growth. I got thrown into it.

I went independent for more room to grow: to learn more, set up more systems and processes, make the decisions and help any department. Today I work as a freelance GTM engineer, building systems that run without the people who used to do them by hand.
