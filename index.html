A little about me

I didn't study growth.
I got thrown into it.

"Most early stage teams don't need five specialists and a bloated agency retainer. They need one person who can audit what's broken and build systems that work."

Rudra Trivedi

Right now

  • Running Firstword, my solo growth ops practice
  • Building AI powered lead & ops automations
  • Taking on a few new freelance projects

I studied AI & Machine Learning, then joined mple.ai as a Product Manager and Founder's Associate. I saw the same pattern again and again: brilliant products, broken systems. Founders were building things people wanted, but had no reliable way to reach them, or wasted months on the wrong channels.

So I became the person who fixes that. I learned marketing by auditing real funnels, outbound by writing thousands of messages and iterating on reply rates, and product by sitting between founders, designers and developers every single day.

Then I automated the slow parts, lead intake, enrichment, CRM sync, AI research briefs, because demand doesn't matter if operations are choking.

Today I freelance with early stage B2B founders and SMBs under Firstword: I audit where you are, find the quick wins, and build repeatable systems. One person, start to finish.
