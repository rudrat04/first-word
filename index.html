My story

I didn't study growth.
I got thrown into it.

Early-stage startups don't have the luxury of time. When I first started working with B2B SaaS founders, I noticed the same pattern: brilliant products, terrible pipelines. Founders were building things people wanted, but nobody knew those things existed — or why they should care.

"Most early-stage teams don't need five specialists and a bloated agency retainer. They need one person who's done the work."

So I became that person. I learned GTM strategy by running it. I learned LinkedIn outbound by writing thousands of messages and iterating on response rates in real-time. I learned product positioning by watching what language made founders' eyes light up — and what made prospects go cold.

Then I automated the parts that were slowing everything down. Invoice generation, lead intake, CRM sync, AI research briefs — because if the pipeline's working but operations are choking, you're still losing.

Today I work directly with early-stage B2B founders as the one operator who moves between positioning, outbound, and automation. No handoffs. No juniors doing your work. Just someone who's seen what breaks first — and knows how to fix it.
