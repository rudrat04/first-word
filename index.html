My story

I didn't study growth.
I got thrown into it.

"Most early-stage teams don't need five specialists and a bloated agency retainer. They need one person who can audit what's broken and build systems that work."

Rudra Trivedi · Operator & Builder

Early-stage startups don't have the luxury of time. When I first started working with B2B SaaS founders, I noticed the same pattern: brilliant products, broken marketing systems. Founders were building things people wanted, but had no reliable strategy to reach them — or wasted months on the wrong channels.

So I became the operator who fixes that. I learned marketing strategy by auditing real funnels, uncovering high-impact findings, and executing quick low-hanging fruit wins. I learned outbound by writing thousands of messages and iterating on response rates in real-time.

Then I automated the parts that were slowing everything down — lead enrichment, intake workflows, CRM sync, and AI research briefs — because if your marketing generates demand but operations are choking, you're still losing.

Today I work directly with early-stage B2B founders to audit their current stage, highlight findings, and build repeatable marketing & automation engines. No handoffs. Just reliable systems from day one.
