1. The Problem

Good leads wait while the rep catches up.

Outbound at volume does not make sense: thousands of emails go to the wrong people, at the wrong time, with no real signal behind them. What closes deals is a rep writing to the right person at the moment something changed.

Meanwhile good leads get missed. A company raises money, opens sales roles or hires a new head of sales and nobody notices. A demo request waits a few hours and goes cold.

"Sales teams do not need more leads. They need the right lead, at the right time, with a real reason to reach out."
2. The Approach

Build the whole loop, keep the human in charge.

01

Find

Watches target companies, discovers new ones each week from public hiring posts and the web, researches any domain, and catches inbound form leads.

02

Qualify

Checks fit (size, stage, region, tools) and buying signals (hiring, funding, news). Each company is labelled Hot, Warm or Watching.

03

Act

Shows who to contact today and why, finds the right person, preps a private brief and alerts within seconds on a hot lead. The rep writes and sends.

04

Close the loop

A one tap "how did it go?" turns two lines of notes into a recap and a follow up draft, and updates HubSpot. HubSpot changes are read back.

6. Decisions Worth Explaining

Why it works the way it does.

✋

Outreach stays manual

The tool never contacts a prospect. It decides who and when, and drafts a suggestion. That keeps it safe, trusted and easy to adopt.

🎯

Fit and signal, not one score

One number mixed "right company" with "good timing" and nobody trusted it. Reps now see Fit and a label: Hot, Warm or Watching.

📏

Rules over AI

Scores, labels and routing are fixed rules, so every decision can be explained. AI writes only short text, like a dossier or a recap.

🗂️

HubSpot stays the record

It writes statuses, deals and notes, and reads back what the rep does there, so nobody has to learn a second tool.
