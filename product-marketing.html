Common questions

What does product marketing include?

Messaging that describes what you sell in your buyers' words, positioning against the alternatives, a go to market plan covering who to target, which channels to use and the first 90 days, and outbound playbooks with ICP segments and tailored sequences.

Can you help with LinkedIn outbound?

Yes. The outbound playbooks cover ICP segments, tailored sequences and tracking so outreach turns into conversations. For mple.ai, rebuilding messaging and segmentation led to 100+ qualified B2B meetings and a 36.5% LinkedIn acceptance rate.

Who is this for?

Teams launching or relaunching a product or service, or whose pitch is not landing with the right buyers.

How does the process work?

Listen (customer calls, your pitch, competitors, numbers), define (ICP, the problem you own, your angle), write (messaging and scripts you can use straight away), then launch with a plan and simple metrics.
