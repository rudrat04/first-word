Marketing Strategy & Audits

From stalled outbound to 100+ qualified B2B meetings

mple.ai had a solid AI product and an energetic team. What they didn't have was a functioning marketing system. Vague ICPs, spray-and-pray sequences, and zero tracking. I audited the bottlenecks, rebuilt the motion from the ground up, and executed it into a predictable pipeline.

Client mple.ai
Service Marketing Strategy, Audits & Outbound
Industry B2B SaaS / AI Productivity
Timeline 3 Months Execution
1. The Problem & Stage Audit

Outbound was happening. Nothing was converting.

When I first audited mple.ai's pipeline, the team wasn't idle. They were sending connection requests, writing follow-ups, and burning hours every week. Yet their conversion into qualified meetings was flatlining.

My stage audit quickly highlighted the core breakdown: their ICP was defined as "anyone who might benefit from AI tools." That lack of specificity meant every outbound message felt like generic spam. Prospects couldn't tell why they should care, and the team had zero conversion tracking to pinpoint where replies died.

"The problem wasn't outreach volume — it was that nobody felt like the message was tailored to their exact pain."
2. What I Did

Rebuilt the marketing system from ICP discovery down.

I began with customer interview audits and sales call analysis to identify the 3 specific verticals that had acute urgency for mple.ai's solution.

Instead of broad blasting, I created tailored messaging architectures for each persona — focusing on their immediate operational headache. Then I established an A/B tested outbound cadence with direct tracking dashboards.

3. The Execution

Step-by-step audit to scalable engine.

01

Stage Audit & ICP Segmentation (Weeks 1–2)

Audited existing messaging and customer calls. Narrowed focus from a generic audience down to 3 high-converting persona segments.

02

Messaging Architecture & Low-Hanging Wins (Week 3)

Drafted bespoke value propositions and short, high-relevance message scripts. Fixed immediate profile positioning gaps.

03

Outbound Launch & Live Tracking (Weeks 4–6)

Launched live campaigns with weekly reply sentiment tracking, acceptance rate optimization, and objection handling.

04

Scale & System Playbook Handoff (Weeks 7–12)

Doubled down on the highest-yielding ICPs, scaling meeting volume to 100+ demos and handing off a documented playbook.

5. What I Took Away

Key insights & honest takeaways.

🎯

Specificity always wins over reach

Cutting out 70% of potential titles to focus on 3 sharp segments tripled positive replies. Broad outreach is false security.

⏱️

Low-hanging fruit fixes build early momentum

Rewriting the first 2 sentences of their outreach generated instant replies within 48 hours while the broader roadmap was being finalized.

📊

Systems beat heroism

Without structured tracking and playbooks, outbound always falls apart when founders get busy. Repeatable systems keep pipeline alive.

💬

Human, concise messaging converts

Messages under 60 words with one clear question outperformed long explanatory paragraphs by over 400%.
