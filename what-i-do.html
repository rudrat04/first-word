02 Product Consultancy

When demos book but don't convert.

Sometimes the outbound is working — meetings are booking — but something's happening in the demo or after it that's killing the deal. That's usually a product problem, not a sales problem.

I come in as a focused second opinion. I look at onboarding flows, feature gaps exposed by customer conversations, and conversion drop-offs — then help you close the loop between what you're promising in the pitch and what you're delivering in the product.

Best for: Teams where growth work is surfacing product gaps — onboarding friction, feature confusion, or demo-to-close leakage you can't fully explain.
What you get
🔍

Onboarding Audit

A clear walkthrough of your activation flow with specific friction points and what to fix first.

🎛️

Feature Prioritization

Helping you build the right thing next — based on what customers actually need vs. what's in the backlog.

💬

Customer Feedback Loops

Setting up structured ways to turn customer conversations into actionable product signals.

📈

Conversion Optimization

Identifying where demos are leaking and what product or process change closes the gap.

How it works
01

Audit Session

02

Gap Analysis

03 Business & AI Automations

The pipeline works. Now let ops keep up.

Growth creates operational load. When outbound starts working, suddenly your team is drowning in manual processes — invoice generation, lead routing, CRM updates, research that takes hours.

I build custom automations for SMBs and B2B teams that remove the manual drag from your operations. Using tools like Make, Zapier, n8n, and custom AI pipelines, I turn hours of busywork into sub-60-second automated workflows.

Best for: SMBs and B2B teams where operations are bottlenecking growth — or where manual processes are burning time that should go into selling or building.
