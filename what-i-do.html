Common questions

What does a freelance GTM engineer do?

I design and build the systems behind a company's sales and marketing: ICP and positioning, outbound, CRM and RevOps setup, and the automations that remove repeat manual work. The goal is a go-to-market motion that runs reliably without constant hand-holding.

Who do you work with?

B2B SaaS teams and small and mid-sized businesses in any industry, from founders running their first outbound to operations teams drowning in manual work. I'm based in Mumbai.

Which services do you offer?

Four: Sales, RevOps and GTM architecture; product marketing and outbound playbooks; website creation; and industry automations.

What is f1rstword?

f1rstword is an account copilot I built for B2B sales. It finds the right companies and people, watches buying signals, scores inbound leads in about 10 seconds and keeps HubSpot in sync. Outreach stays human.

How do I get started?
