Common questions

How is this different from a normal website?

Most sites stop at a contact form that lands in an inbox. Every site I build ends in a working lead flow: forms or booking wired to your CRM, the lead source tracked, and a first follow-up sent automatically.

Do you write the copy too?

Yes. Messaging comes first, so the copy explains your offer clearly before the design starts.

How much does a website cost?

A launch site of up to five pages starts at ₹30,000 for Indian clients and $800 for international clients. A growth site of up to ten pages, with booking, routing and analytics, starts at ₹60,000 or $1,500. Domain and hosting are paid by you, directly to the vendor.

Do you build online stores?
